Our goals

What drives us every day

Four clear goals that guide every game, every line of code and every studio decision.

01

Make gaming accessible to everyone

Tear down the barriers of traditional gaming: no pricey hardware, no endless downloads. If you have a browser, you have a full axcStudios console.

02

Build original sagas with identity

We don't live off copying: Kingdom of the Forgotten Seas, Zona Cero and Grand Theft Voxel are born here, with their own rules, aesthetic and universes that grow release after release.

03

Push the web to the limit

The web is our console: real-time 3D graphics, accounts with real verification and games that boot in seconds on any device, with nothing to install.

04

Keep the ecosystem free

Ten free games today, more tomorrow. Our model stands on community and the passion to create, not on the pockets of those who just want to play.
